## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Danylo understands the paid trial as a diagnostic first step that clarifies level, goal, weak spots, and format.

**User Situation:** Danylo arrives from the Exam Preparation Path after confirming that Fluyo covers his exam and that preparation is structured.

**Success Criteria:** Danylo understands what happens during the first paid trial, what the teacher checks, how a learning plan is created, and why pricing/formats are the next logical step.

**Entry Points:**
- Exam Preparation Path CTA
- Navigation menu
- Direct anchor link to `#how-learning-works`

**Exit Points:**
- Continue to Programs and Pricing
- Open Telegram trial booking
- Return to Exam Preparation Path if he wants to re-check exam fit
