## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Danylo compares the trial, individual, pair, and mini-group options, then clicks Telegram ready to book.

**User Situation:** Danylo arrives after understanding the learning process and now needs price/format clarity before taking the final paid-trial step.

**Success Criteria:** The visitor can compare available formats, understand the paid trial as the first step, see what each option includes, and open Telegram with enough context to book or ask a specific pricing question.

**Entry Points:**
- How Learning Works CTA
- Hero "View programs" CTA
- Navigation menu
- Direct anchor link to `#programs-pricing`

**Exit Points:**
- Final Telegram booking click
- Telegram pricing question
- Continue scrolling into trust/proof/FAQ sections if present later in the page

## Page States

| State | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| Default / loaded | Final prices and content are approved. | Header, trial card, format cards, included-value items, and CTAs are visible. | Visitor can compare formats and open Telegram. |
| Pricing tracked | Final prices or trial duration are outside public scope. | Implementation note is captured in project artifacts; production must not show final content prices as final. | Resolve pricing before launch or hide/replace affected values. |
| Loading | Pricing data is loaded from config/CMS. | Stable card layout with skeleton price values only if dynamic pricing is used. | CTAs may remain usable only if pricing is already known or not required for booking. |
| Error | Pricing config/CMS fails. | Do not show stale or unavailable prices; show neutral fallback or block publish. | Telegram CTA may remain if copy does not imply exact price. |
| Success | User opens Telegram. | External chat opens; section itself does not show confirmation. | User can return and continue browsing. |
