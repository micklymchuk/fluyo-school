## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Danylo confirms his exam is covered and sees that exam preparation is structured, not generic.

**User Situation:** Danylo arrives from the Landing Page / Hero by selecting the exam path, using the scroll hint, opening the navigation menu, or landing on a direct anchor link.

**Success Criteria:** Danylo recognizes his exam or knows he can ask about it, understands the preparation method is diagnostic and plan-based, and continues toward How Learning Works or Telegram with exam context.

**Entry Points:**
- Hero path card
- Hero continuation scroll hint
- Navigation menu
- Direct anchor link to `#exam-preparation`

**Exit Points:**
- Continue to How Learning Works
- Ask about an exam through Telegram
- Book through Telegram if ready
- Continue later to Programs and Pricing

## Page States

| State | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| Default / loaded | Section content is ready. | Header copy, exam cards, method steps, proof area, and CTAs are visible. | Select exam card, ask via Telegram, continue to How Learning Works. |
| Loading | Visual/proof asset is loading. | Text and exam cards render; flow/proof visual uses final content. | CTAs and exam cards remain usable. |
| Empty proof | No approved result/testimonial exists yet. | Content-gap-safe final content or omitted proof card in production. | Continue CTA and Telegram remain available. |
| Error | Visual/proof asset fails. | Section remains intact; visual area uses neutral fallback. | Exam cards and CTAs remain available. |
| Success | User selects exam card or opens Telegram. | Selected card stays marked; Telegram opens externally. | User can change selected exam or continue scrolling. |
