## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Danylo recognizes Fluyo as a serious teacher-led English school and sees the trial CTA plus a trust signal.

**User Situation:** Danylo opens the landing page from mobile search or Instagram after looking for exam-prep English help.

**Success Criteria:** The visitor understands Fluyo is a live teacher-led English school, sees a clear paid-trial CTA, can switch language, and can move toward exam preparation or programs/pricing without friction.

**Entry Points:**
- Mobile search
- Instagram
- Shared link

**Exit Points:**
- Scroll to Exam Preparation Path
- Scroll to Audience Path Preview
- Scroll to Programs and Pricing
- Open Telegram trial-booking chat
- Open Instagram profile

---

## First-Step Context

**User Situation:** Danylo is a university applicant studying from his phone between evening practice tasks, six weeks before an English exam that affects admission. He has been using YouTube and practice tests, but now feels he needs a teacher to diagnose his weak spots and build a realistic plan.

**Mental State:**
- **Trigger:** A frustrating practice test six weeks before the exam makes Danylo realize scattered self-study is not enough.
- **Hope:** Find proof that Fluyo understands his exact exam and can turn his current level into a clear preparation plan.
- **Worry:** Waste precious weeks on pleasant but generic lessons that do not improve his exam readiness.

**Discovery Method:** Danylo searches on mobile for exam-prep English help after a frustrating practice test, sees Fluyo in search or Instagram, and opens the landing page to check whether it covers his exact exam.

## Page States

| State | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| Default / loaded | Page assets and text are ready. | Full header, hero, audience paths, CTAs, and continuation cue are visible. | All navigation, language toggle, Instagram, Telegram, and scroll anchors are available. |
| Loading | Fonts/images are still loading. | Text renders immediately; hero image shows skeleton or soft placeholder. | Header links and text CTAs remain usable when possible. |
| Error | Hero visual or external asset fails. | Layout remains intact; image area shows neutral fallback and no blocking page error. | CTAs, path cards, language toggle, and navigation remain usable. |
| Empty | No dynamic content available. | Not applicable for static hero; use approved fallback copy if future CMS content is missing. | Static fallback copy remains. |
| Success | User clicks Telegram or Instagram. | External app/tab opens; landing page itself does not show a success confirmation. | User can return and continue browsing. |

## Technical Notes

- Default language is Ukrainian; English is available through the `UA / EN` switcher.
- Browser SEO title is separate from the internal page title and should be defined during meta-content work.
- Telegram username is confirmed as `fluyo_manager`.
- Instagram URL is confirmed as `https://www.instagram.com/fluyo.school`.
- `View programs` intentionally goes to the later Programs and Pricing section, not the Audience Path Preview.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for smooth scrolling and any scroll-hint motion.
- The hero trust line intentionally avoids combining goals and audiences; audience segmentation is handled by the path cards.
