## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Olena reviews screenshots or examples showing interactive online lessons and parent-visible feedback.

**User Situation:** Olena has seen that Fluyo speaks to parents and has real teacher proof; now she needs to see whether online lessons are active enough for a child and understandable enough for a parent.

**Success Criteria:** Olena sees concrete proof of lesson activity: visual materials, speaking prompts, interaction moments, teacher feedback, and a parent-facing progress update pattern before she continues to final social proof and FAQ.

**Entry Points:**
- Teachers section CTA
- Kids / Parents Path engagement card if interactive
- Navigation menu
- Direct anchor link to `#lesson-experience`

**Exit Points:**
- Continue to Results / Testimonials / FAQ / Final CTA
- Ask about a child's lesson through Telegram
- Open or zoom approved lesson screenshots/material examples if available
