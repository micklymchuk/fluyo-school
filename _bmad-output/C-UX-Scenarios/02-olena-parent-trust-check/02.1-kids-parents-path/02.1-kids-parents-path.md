## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Olena sees that Fluyo speaks directly to parents choosing lessons for children 6+ and understands child motivation.

**User Situation:** Olena arrives from an Instagram post, another parent's shared link, the Audience Paths section, navigation menu, or a direct anchor while comparing online English options for her child.

**Success Criteria:** Olena feels the kids path is built for parents as buyers and children as learners: safe teacher tone, engaging online lessons, parent-visible progress, and a clear next step into teacher proof.

**Entry Points:**
- Audience Path Preview kids card
- Navigation menu
- Instagram
- Shared parent link
- Direct anchor link to `#kids-parents`

**Exit Points:**
- Continue to Teachers
- Ask about a child's lesson through Telegram
- Continue scrolling toward Lesson Experience and FAQ later in the page

---

## First-Step Context

**User Situation:** Olena is a mother comparing online English options on her phone after her child complained that a previous lesson felt boring. She wants a teacher who feels warm and competent, but she also needs to know online lessons will be active enough to hold her child's attention.

**Mental State:**
- **Trigger:** Her child complains that a previous online English lesson felt boring.
- **Hope:** See real teachers, engaging child lesson examples, and clear progress reassurance before she pays for a trial.
- **Worry:** Choose an online lesson that her child dislikes, ignores, or abandons after one attempt.

**Discovery Method:** Olena opens Fluyo from an Instagram post or another parent's shared link while comparing children's online English options on her phone.
