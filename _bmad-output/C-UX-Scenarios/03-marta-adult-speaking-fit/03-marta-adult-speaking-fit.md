# 03: Marta's Adult Speaking Fit

**Project:** fluyo-school
**Created:** 2026-06-25
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Transaction (Q1)

**What this scenario covers:**
Decide Fluyo feels like a practical, non-judgmental way to start speaking English again.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** Tertiary Goal: Make Each Launch Audience Feel Immediately Recognized
**Objective:** Audience-path engagement and path-specific CTA coverage for adults from the Trigger Map.

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Marta the Momentum-Seeking Adult (Tertiary)
**Situation:** Marta is an adult learner scrolling on her phone in the evening after freezing during a short English conversation at work. She knows grammar passively but feels embarrassed that she still cannot speak naturally, so she is looking for a teacher-led path that feels practical and safe.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Trigger:** Marta freezes during a short English conversation at work and feels the gap between what she knows and what she can say.

**Hope:** Find a respectful adult speaking path where mistakes are corrected supportively and lessons connect to real situations.

**Worry:** Start another course, freeze during live speaking, and feel like she has failed at English again.

> CONSTRAINT: One sentence per component. Phrases, not paragraphs.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile
**Entry:** Marta opens Fluyo on mobile from Instagram or a friend's recommendation after searching for online English lessons focused on speaking confidence.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:**
Marta recognizes herself in the adult path, sees practical speaking topics and supportive teacher tone, understands why Fluyo feels manageable, and continues toward Telegram with a real-life speaking goal.

**Business Success:**
Fluyo earns adult audience-path engagement and a qualified speaking-focused trial lead that broadens demand beyond exams and kids.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Audience Paths** — Marta sees Adults as one of the three clear launch paths and chooses it without feeling the site is only for exams or children.
2. **Adults Path** — She recognizes her speaking anxiety and sees practical situations like work, travel, study, and everyday conversation.
3. **Why Fluyo** — She sees supportive teachers, speaking focus, personalized path, flexible formats, and modern materials as reasons this can be manageable. ✓
