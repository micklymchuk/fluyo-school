## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Marta sees Adults as one of the three clear launch paths and chooses it without feeling the site is only for exams or children.

**User Situation:** Marta arrives from Instagram, a friend's recommendation, the hero, or a direct landing page visit after freezing during a short English conversation and looking for a practical speaking path.

**Success Criteria:** Marta can scan the three launch paths, recognize that adults are a first-class audience, understand that the adult path is about speaking confidence and real-life situations, and continue to the Adults Path without shame or friction.

**Entry Points:**
- Hero path review
- Header or mobile menu navigation
- Instagram
- Friend recommendation
- Direct anchor link to `#audience-paths`

**Exit Points:**
- Adults Path
- Exam Preparation Path
- Kids / Parents Path
- Telegram with adult speaking goal context

---

## First-Step Context

**User Situation:** Marta is an adult learner scrolling on her phone in the evening after freezing during a short English conversation at work. She knows grammar passively but feels embarrassed that she still cannot speak naturally, so she is looking for a teacher-led path that feels practical and safe.

**Mental State:**
- **Trigger:** Marta freezes during a short English conversation at work and feels the gap between what she knows and what she can say.
- **Hope:** Find a respectful adult speaking path where mistakes are corrected supportively and lessons connect to real situations.
- **Worry:** Start another course, freeze during live speaking, and feel like she has failed at English again.

**Discovery Method:** Marta opens Fluyo on mobile from Instagram or a friend's recommendation after searching for online English lessons focused on speaking confidence.

| Success | User reaches a destination section or opens Telegram. | Page scrolls or external chat opens; no inline success confirmation required. | User can return and continue browsing. |
