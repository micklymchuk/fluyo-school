## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Marta recognizes her speaking anxiety and sees practical situations like work, travel, study, and everyday conversation.

**User Situation:** Marta arrives from Audience Paths after choosing Adults, already reassured that Fluyo is not only for exams or children.

**Success Criteria:** Marta sees that adult lessons are speaking-focused, respectful, practical, and flexible enough to fit her life, then continues to Why Fluyo or opens Telegram with an adult speaking goal.

**Entry Points:**
- Audience Paths Adults card
- Hero adult path card
- Header or mobile menu navigation
- Direct anchor link to `#adults`
- Instagram or referral link that scrolls to the adult section

**Exit Points:**
- Continue to Why Fluyo
- Open Telegram with adult speaking goal context
- Continue to Programs and Pricing if she needs format or price clarity
- Return to Audience Paths if she wants to compare paths

---

## Scenario Continuity

**Previous Step Context:** Marta selected Adults because she saw that Fluyo has a first-class adult path, not a small extra program hidden behind exam and kids messaging.

**Mental State Carried In:**
- **Trigger:** She froze during a short English conversation and felt the gap between passive knowledge and live speech.
- **Hope:** She wants practical speaking practice with supportive correction.
- **Worry:** She does not want another course that makes her feel slow, exposed, or childish.

**Design Intent:** This section should feel adult, calm, and non-patronizing. It should name the problem directly without making Marta feel blamed for it.
