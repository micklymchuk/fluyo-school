## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Marta sees supportive teachers, speaking focus, personalized path, flexible formats, and modern materials as reasons Fluyo can feel manageable.

**User Situation:** Marta has recognized herself in the Adults Path and now needs credible reasons to believe Fluyo is practical, supportive, and worth contacting.

**Success Criteria:** Marta understands why Fluyo is different enough from past failed attempts, sees concise proof-backed reasons, and opens Telegram ready to describe a real-life speaking goal.

**Entry Points:**
- Adults Path CTA
- Header or mobile menu navigation
- Hero value-prop scroll
- Direct anchor link to `#why-fluyo`
- Returning visitor who wants school-level differentiation before booking

**Exit Points:**
- Final Telegram booking click with adult speaking goal context
- Programs and Pricing if Marta wants format or price detail
- Teachers or Lesson Experience if she wants deeper proof
- Instagram if she wants additional social proof outside the site

---

## Scenario Continuity

**Previous Step Context:** Marta saw that adult lessons are about real situations, safe speaking practice, gentle correction, and a rhythm that can survive a busy schedule.

**Mental State Carried In:**
- **Hope:** This might be a school where speaking practice feels adult, practical, and human.
- **Worry:** The page may still be making generic promises without proof or a clear first step.
- **Decision Need:** She needs enough confidence to send a message without feeling trapped in another course.

**Design Intent:** Keep this section concise and proof-backed. It should explain differentiation without becoming a generic "benefits" block or duplicating the full Teachers, Lesson Experience, and Pricing sections.
