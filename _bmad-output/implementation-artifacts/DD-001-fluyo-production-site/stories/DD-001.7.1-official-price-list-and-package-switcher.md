# DD-001.7.1: Official Price List And Package Switcher

**Epic:** 7 - Pricing reconciliation
**Status:** review
**Size:** Large
**Depends On:** `DD-001.3.2` (trial-pricing route), `DD-001.5.2` (brand section rewiring)
**Source:** School's official Instagram "Price list" story set (14 July), reconciled to the live data-driven pricing system.

## Context / Goal

The school finalized its official price list. The live site's pricing no longer matches it and lacks two concepts the list introduces: **multi-lesson packages** and **special-condition discounts**. Fully reconcile the site's pricing data + pages with the list (numbers/structure, not the story's visual style), and turn the home pricing summary into an interactive **package-size switcher**.

We extend the existing data-driven machinery (single source of truth `app/data/content/pricing.ts`, i18n currency via `usePriceValue`, `UiCard` tier cards) — no parallel systems.

### Official price list (source of truth)

Single lessons (price per person): General English `INDIVIDUAL` 575₴/60m · Exam prep `EXAM_PREP` 675₴/60m · **Pair `PAIR` (new)** 400₴/60m · Group `MINI_GROUP` (up to 4) 350₴/75m · Trial `TRIAL` free/30m.

Packages (per-lesson drops with size; 8 = most popular):
- **General English:** 4 → 2300 (575/ea) · 8 → 4400 (550/ea) ✦ · 12 → 6300 (525/ea)
- **Exam prep:** 4 → 2700 (675/ea) · 8 → 5200 (650/ea) ✦ · 12 → 7500 (625/ea)

Special conditions: 10% off first package purchase · 10% off military families (all packages, doc may be required).

### Decisions locked with the user
1. Full reconciliation.
2. **Remove Speaking club from *pricing*** (drop `SPEAKING_CLUB` price item + `speaking-club` pricing *format*) — **keep** the speaking-club *learning path*, its home section, and telegram context.
3. **Exact** single prices (no "від/from"); packages show the reduced per-lesson rate.
4. **Home** pricing summary becomes a **package-size switcher** (1/4/8/12) that recomputes prices per selection.
