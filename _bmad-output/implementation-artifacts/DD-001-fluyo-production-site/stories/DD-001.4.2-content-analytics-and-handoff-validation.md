## Error And Edge Case Checks

- `/pricing?path=unknown` falls back safely.
- Generic Telegram CTA works without path, format, or message intent.
- Unsupported locale falls back to Ukrainian.
- Direct Learning Paths anchors are readable after header offset.
- Direct pricing query links preserve or safely fallback path context.
- Rapid CTA clicks keep links valid and tracking payloads well-formed.
- Long Ukrainian and English strings wrap cleanly.
- Optional missing proof images, teacher portraits, or screenshots do not break layout.
- Mobile menu state remains coherent across navigation, locale switching, and browser history.

## Analytics Contract

- `price_preview_view`
- `pricing_view`
- `teacher_proof_view`
- `telegram_click`
- `telegram_context`

Required payload fields when available:

- `route`
- `locale`
- `path`
- `format`
- `sourceRoute`
- `messageIntent`

## Launch Content Validation

- Prices and trial terms are approved, hidden, or blocking.
- Teacher profiles, portraits, credentials, testimonials, results, lesson screenshots, and proof assets are approved, hidden, or blocking.
- Policy-sensitive FAQ answers are approved or omitted.
- Prepared Telegram messages are approved, deferred behind direct fallback, or blocking.
- No visible `mock_*` ids or mock records render as public truth in production mode.
