# Product Brief: Fluyo School Landing Page

## Executive Summary

Fluyo School is a modern online English school for students who want teacher-led learning that feels natural, supportive, and practical. The brand idea is "Fluent + Flow": English should not feel heavy or intimidating; students should feel that they are moving smoothly toward real communication.

The landing page should present Fluyo as premium but approachable. It needs to convert visitors into paid trial lesson bookings through Telegram while giving enough trust, clarity, and proof for people to feel safe taking the first step.

The first version should focus on three primary audience paths: exam preparation, parents/kids, and adults. Other segments can be present, but these three should shape the page structure, proof, and calls to action.

## Primary Page Goal

Primary conversion:

- Book a paid trial lesson through Telegram.

Secondary actions:

- View programs
- Meet teachers
- Explore direct pricing
- Review proof: certificates, screenshots, testimonials, and results

The page should answer four questions quickly:

- What is Fluyo School?
- Is this right for my goal or my child?
- Can I trust these teachers?
- How do I book the first paid trial lesson?

## Audience Strategy

The page should support three launch audiences:

1. Exam preparation students
   Students preparing for NMT, EVI, Cambridge, TOEFL, CELPIP, and similar goals. They need structure, deadlines, credibility, teacher expertise, and proof of results.

2. Parents and kids
   Parents looking for online English lessons for children 6+. They need safety, teacher warmth, age-appropriate materials, visible progress, and confidence that the child will enjoy lessons.

3. Adults
   Adults who want to speak more confidently for everyday life, travel, work, studies, or personal growth. They need practical speaking, flexible schedule, clear formats, and a low-pressure way to start.

[ASSUMPTION] The hero can speak broadly to confident, natural English, then immediately route visitors into these three paths through program cards or tabs.

## Positioning

Fluyo School is an elegant, human online English school for real-life progress with teachers.

Core promise:

Learn English in flow: with supportive teachers, practical lessons, and a path built around your goal.

What makes Fluyo different:

- Personalized learning paths
- Speaking-focused lessons
- Friendly, supportive teachers
- Small groups, pairs, and individual formats
- Real-life English instead of textbook-only learning
- Exam preparation with visible goals
- Modern online materials and lesson tools
- Flexible schedule
- Comfortable learning from anywhere

The tone should avoid exaggerated guarantees. The school should sound confident, warm, and precise.

## Offer And Programs

Known offer categories:

- Exam preparation
- Kids English
- Adult English
- Teens
- Business English
- Individual lessons
- Pairs
- Mini-groups

Pricing should be shown directly, not hidden behind contact. The pricing section should make formats easy to compare and should include the paid trial lesson as a clear first step.

production pricing for design and implementation:

- Paid trial lesson: 30 minutes, 300 UAH
- Individual lesson: 60 minutes, from 700 UAH
- Pair lesson: 60 minutes, from 500 UAH per student
- Mini-group lesson: 60 minutes, from 350 UAH per student
- Mini-group size: up to 4 students

[CONFIRMED] Mini-groups are capped at 4 students.

[ASSUMPTION] Prices and paid trial lesson duration are defined for production. The values above are production content for layout/design only and must be replaced before public launch.

## Trust And Proof

Available proof assets:

- Teacher photos
- Certificates
- Lesson screenshots
- Testimonials
- Student results

[CONFIRMED] All listed proof asset categories are approved for public use.

These assets should not be buried. They should appear throughout the page:

- Hero or near-hero: one concise trust signal
- Teacher section: portraits, credentials, short bios
- Lesson experience section: screenshots of online lessons or materials
- Exam section: certificates and results
- Social proof section: testimonials from students or parents
- Pricing / CTA area: reassurance before booking

The page should show enough real material to avoid feeling like a beautiful but unproven brand.

## Design Direction

Desired feeling:

- Modern
- Premium
- Clean
- Minimalistic
- Friendly
- Inspiring
- Trustworthy

Core palette:

- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Charcoal black: `#1A1A1A`
- Deep burgundy: `#620F13`

Visual language:

- Elegant whitespace
- Clean typography
- Smooth scrolling and subtle motion
- Rounded shapes used with restraint
- Real teacher/student imagery
- Custom 3D icons
- Wax-stamp style elements as a signature brand motif

Avoid:

- Busy layouts
- Bright rainbow education colors
- Cartoon-style illustrations
- Generic stock imagery
- Too much text on one screen
- Overly corporate SaaS styling

## Language And Localization

The landing page should support both Ukrainian and English.

[CONFIRMED] Ukrainian should be the default language, with English available through a language switcher.

The design must account for text expansion between languages and avoid hard-coded layouts that break when labels, pricing, or testimonials are translated.

production Telegram flow:

- Primary CTA label in English: "Book a trial lesson"
- Primary CTA label in Ukrainian: "Записатися на пробний урок"
- CTA destination: `https://t.me/fluyo_manager`
- Prepared message: "Hi! I want to book a paid trial lesson at Fluyo School."

[CONFIRMED] The Telegram username is `fluyo_manager`.

## Recommended Page Structure

1. Hero
   Clear promise, short explanation, primary Telegram trial CTA, and one proof signal.

2. Audience paths
   Three cards or tabs: Exam Preparation, Kids, Adults. Each should lead to a tailored explanation and CTA.

3. Why Fluyo
   Speaking focus, personalized path, supportive teachers, small groups, modern digital materials.

4. How learning works
   Placement / goal check, format selection, paid trial lesson, teacher plan, regular lessons.

5. Teachers
   Real portraits, certificates, short credentials, and teaching style.

6. Lesson experience
   Screenshots or visuals showing online lessons, interactive tasks, digital materials, and communication.

7. Programs and pricing
   Direct prices, lesson formats, group sizes, trial lesson price, and what is included.

8. Results and testimonials
   Student results, parent feedback, screenshots or short quotes where approved.

9. FAQ
   Trial lesson, payment, schedule, levels, formats, kids lessons, exam prep, cancellation/rescheduling.

10. Final CTA
   Telegram booking plus secondary contact options if available.

## Success Criteria

The landing page is working if:

- Visitors understand the school and target audiences within the first screen.
- Visitors can choose their path: exams, kids, or adults.
- The paid trial lesson feels clear and low-friction.
- Teacher credibility and real proof are visible before pricing.
- Pricing is understandable without contacting the manager.
- The site feels premium and warm, not childish or generic.
- The bilingual version feels native in both Ukrainian and English.
