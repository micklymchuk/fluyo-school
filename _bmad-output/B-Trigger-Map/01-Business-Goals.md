# Business Goals & Objectives

> Strategic goals and measurable objectives for Fluyo School's landing page

**Document:** Trigger Map - Business Goals  
**Created:** 2026-06-25  
**Status:** Approved  
**Mode:** Suggest  

---

## Vision

Fluyo School should become the trusted, premium-feeling first step for Ukrainian learners and parents who want English to feel natural, practical, and supported by real teachers.

The landing page should turn interest into paid trial lesson bookings by making each visitor quickly feel: "This is the right school for my goal, I understand the first step, and I trust the people behind it."

---

## Business Objectives

### Primary Goal: Create Confident Paid Trial Starters (THE ENGINE)

**Statement:** Help qualified visitors from the three launch audiences feel ready to book a paid trial lesson through a clear Telegram-first path.

**Why this is the engine:** The paid trial lesson is the first real relationship moment. If visitors understand the school, trust the teachers, and know what the trial does, they can naturally become trial students who create the proof and learning stories that strengthen future demand.

**SMART Objectives:**

1. **Trial CTA engagement**
   - **Metric:** Visitor-to-Telegram CTA click rate
   - **Target:** 8-12% of qualified landing-page visitors
   - **Timeline:** First 90 days after launch
   - **Impact:** Shows whether the promise, path routing, and CTA clarity create enough confidence to start the booking conversation.

2. **Paid trial booking rate**
   - **Metric:** Telegram trial inquiries that become paid trial bookings
   - **Target:** 35-50% of qualified Telegram inquiries
   - **Timeline:** First 90 days after launch
   - **Impact:** Measures whether the page attracts good-fit visitors and pre-answers enough questions before they contact the manager.

3. **Qualified inquiry quality**
   - **Metric:** Share of Telegram inquiries that mention a program path, learner goal, age/level, or preferred lesson format
   - **Target:** 70%+ of trial inquiries include at least one qualifying detail
   - **Timeline:** First 60 days after launch
   - **Impact:** Reduces manager friction and shows that visitors understand their next step before contact.

---

### Secondary Goal: Build Trust Before The Visitor Compares Price

**Statement:** Make Fluyo feel credible, human, and safe before visitors reach pricing or open Telegram.

**Driven by the engine:** Paid trial starters need evidence before they act. Teacher credibility, lesson visibility, and real proof help visitors feel that the trial is worth paying for.

**SMART Objectives:**

1. **Proof visibility**
   - **Metric:** Engaged visitors who view at least one trust/proof section before clicking Telegram
   - **Target:** 60%+ of CTA clickers
   - **Timeline:** First 90 days after launch
   - **Impact:** Ensures booking confidence is supported by credibility, not only by a polished hero.

2. **Launch proof inventory**
   - **Metric:** Public proof assets integrated into the page
   - **Target:** At least 3 teacher profiles, 3 certificate/result examples, 3 testimonials or parent/student quotes, and 2 lesson screenshots
   - **Timeline:** Before public launch
   - **Impact:** Prevents the page from feeling premium but unproven.

3. **Trust-section engagement**
   - **Metric:** Visitors who reach or interact with teacher, lesson experience, results, or testimonials sections
   - **Target:** 45%+ of engaged visitors
   - **Timeline:** First 90 days after launch
   - **Impact:** Shows whether proof is visible and compelling enough to support the paid first step.

---

### Tertiary Goal: Make Each Launch Audience Feel Immediately Recognized

**Statement:** Help exam students, parents, and adults quickly find the path that matches their goal and see a relevant reason to book.

**Benefit for learners and parents:** Visitors should not have to decode whether Fluyo is "for them." Each group should see its goal, its worries, and its next step reflected clearly.

**SMART Objectives:**

1. **Audience-path engagement**
   - **Metric:** Visitors who select, scroll to, or interact with one of the three primary program paths
   - **Target:** 55%+ of engaged visitors
   - **Timeline:** First 90 days after launch
   - **Impact:** Confirms that visitors understand where they fit: exams, kids, or adults.

2. **Path-specific CTA coverage**
   - **Metric:** Primary audience paths with tailored proof, objections, and Telegram CTAs
   - **Target:** 3 of 3 launch paths fully covered
   - **Timeline:** Before public launch
   - **Impact:** Ensures every major visitor type gets a specific reason to book, not only a generic school promise.

3. **Bilingual clarity**
   - **Metric:** Critical UA/EN page sections reviewed for native readability and layout fit
   - **Target:** 100% of hero, program paths, pricing, proof, FAQ, and CTA sections pass review in Ukrainian and English
   - **Timeline:** Before public launch
   - **Impact:** Protects the confirmed Ukrainian-default strategy and avoids trust loss from awkward translation or broken layouts.

## The Flywheel: How Goals Connect

**THE ENGINE:** Confident paid trial starters.

1. Visitors recognize their path: exams, kids, or adults.
2. They see teacher proof, lesson proof, pricing clarity, and trial clarity.
3. They message Fluyo through Telegram with a specific goal.
4. Good-fit trials become regular learners.
5. Regular learners create real proof: testimonials, exam results, parent feedback, screenshots, and teacher credibility.
6. That proof makes the next visitor feel safer and more ready to book.

```text
Audience recognition
    -> trust before price
    -> confident paid trial starter
    -> regular learner
    -> visible proof
    -> stronger trust for the next visitor
```

## Success Metrics Alignment

### Danylo the Deadline-Driven Exam Student

- **Primary Goal:** His urgency makes him the strongest paid-trial starter when exam proof and structure are clear.
- **Trust Goal:** He needs exam-specific teacher credibility, results, and a diagnostic trial.
- **Audience Goal:** He must see NMT, EVI, Cambridge, TOEFL, CELPIP, or similar exam paths quickly.

### Olena the Observant Parent

- **Primary Goal:** She books when the trial feels safe and useful for her child.
- **Trust Goal:** She needs teacher warmth, parent feedback, child engagement proof, and visible progress.
- **Audience Goal:** The kids path must speak to her as the buyer while showing the child's lesson experience.

### Marta the Momentum-Seeking Adult

- **Primary Goal:** She books when the first step feels practical and non-intimidating.
- **Trust Goal:** She needs supportive teacher tone, speaking practice proof, and realistic progress language.
- **Audience Goal:** The adult path must name real-life speaking, work, travel, and everyday confidence.

## Assumptions To Confirm

- Exact paid trial lesson price and duration remain unresolved.
- Exact prices for individual, pair, and mini-group lessons remain unresolved.
- Traffic baseline is not available yet, so targets are proposed starting targets.
- Analytics events need to be defined during implementation to measure CTA clicks, audience-path engagement, proof-section engagement, and inquiry quality.
- Telegram is treated as the primary contact channel unless a secondary channel is later confirmed.
