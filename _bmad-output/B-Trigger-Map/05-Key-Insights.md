## The Flywheel: Confident Trial Starters Drive Everything

**THE ENGINE:**

- Confident paid trial starters are the primary goal.
- Timeline: first 90 days after launch.
- These visitors understand their path, trust the teachers, and know what the paid trial gives them.
- They create the flywheel that drives all other objectives.

**Trust Before Price:**

- Driven by proof that makes the paid first step feel justified.
- Teacher profiles, lesson screenshots, certificates/results, and testimonials must appear before or around pricing.
- Timeline: before public launch, then measured during first 90 days.
- Focus: reduce perceived risk before Telegram.

**Audience Recognition:**

- Real benefits for learners and parents.
- Danylo sees exam seriousness.
- Olena sees child safety and progress.
- Marta sees practical speaking without shame.
- Timeline: before public launch.

---

## Primary Development Focus

1. **Create awesome exam-prep trial starters** - Danylo is the primary profile who needs urgency, exam proof, and structure.
2. **Make the paid trial feel valuable** - clarify what happens in the trial and why it is worth paying for.
3. **Prove teacher trust fast** - real faces, credentials, warmth, and teaching style matter more than decorative polish.
4. **Give each audience a clear path** - exams, kids, and adults must be visible without making the page feel busy.
5. **Preserve bilingual credibility** - Ukrainian-default copy must feel native and English must not break layout or trust.

---

## Critical Success Factors

- **Exam specificity:** Danylo must see NMT, EVI, Cambridge, TOEFL, CELPIP, or equivalent exam language quickly.
- **Teacher credibility:** every path depends on trust in real teachers.
- **Trial clarity:** visitors need to know what happens before, during, and after the paid trial.
- **Proof before price:** proof assets must not be buried below pricing.
- **Direct pricing:** hidden pricing would increase anxiety for all personas.
- **Path-aware Telegram:** the contact flow should preserve whether the visitor is asking about exams, a child, or adult speaking.

---

## Design Implications

### Hero Must:

- State the broad Fluyo promise clearly.
- Include the Telegram trial CTA.
- Avoid trying to explain every program in the headline.
- Hint at teacher-led trust and practical progress.

### Audience Paths Must:

- Route immediately into Exam Preparation, Kids, and Adults.
- Give Danylo the sharpest proof and structure.
- Speak to Olena as the decision maker for the child.
- Let Marta recognize speaking anxiety and practical goals.

### Exam Path Must:

- Name specific exams.
- Explain structure and preparation rhythm.
- Show teacher credibility and results.
- Make the trial feel diagnostic.

### Kids Path Must:

- Show teacher warmth.
- Show child-appropriate online lesson experience.
- Explain parent-visible progress.
- Make trial fit feel gentle and safe.

### Adults Path Must:

- Emphasize speaking without judgment.
- Name real-life contexts: work, travel, study, everyday communication.
- Show flexible formats.
- Avoid childish or overly academic tone.

### Proof Sections Must:

- Use teacher portraits, credentials, certificates, testimonials, student results, and lesson screenshots.
- Match proof to path where possible.
- Appear before the visitor is asked to compare price seriously.

### Pricing And CTA Must:

- Show paid trial price and duration once confirmed.
- Show individual, pair, and mini-group formats.
- State mini-groups are capped at four students.
- Use Telegram as the primary booking action.

## Design Focus Statement

Design the Fluyo School landing page primarily for **Danylo the Deadline-Driven Exam Student**, while strongly supporting **Olena the Observant Parent** and keeping **Marta the Momentum-Seeking Adult** visible as the broad confidence path.

The page must transform visitors from "I am interested, but I need proof and clarity before I pay for a trial" into "I understand my path, I trust the teachers, I know what the trial does, and I am ready to message Fluyo on Telegram."

**Must Address:**

1. Paid trial clarity.
2. Exam-specific credibility.
3. Teacher trust.
4. Path-specific proof.
5. Transparent pricing and formats.

**Should Address:**

1. Parent progress communication.
2. Lesson screenshots and material examples.
3. Adult real-life topics.
4. Level and format matching.
5. Bilingual UA/EN content quality.

## Development Phases

### First Deliverable: Fluyo School Landing Page MVP

Focus on the proof-led conversion spine:

- **Hero** - broad promise, Telegram CTA, one trust signal.
- **Audience paths** - Exams, Kids, Adults.
- **Exam path** - strongest primary persona section.
- **How learning works** - paid trial, goal check, teacher plan, regular lessons.
- **Teacher proof** - real portraits, credentials, teaching style.
- **Lesson experience** - screenshots or material examples.
- **Programs and pricing** - direct format comparison.
- **Results and testimonials** - segmented proof where possible.
- **FAQ and final CTA** - reassurance before action.

### Future Phases

- **Phase 2:** Deeper Business English and Teens content if demand warrants it.
- **Phase 3:** Separate SEO pages for exam prep, kids English, adult speaking, and business English.
- **Phase 4:** Richer teacher galleries and more detailed lesson/material previews.
- **Phase 5:** More automated or context-aware Telegram booking messages if validated.
